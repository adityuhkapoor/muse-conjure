# Skills

One job per file. The factory routes by these lines; a person can read any file on its own.

- `reading-the-moment.md`: always first; pull the job, decision shape, people, sourced facts, constraints, gaps, tone and surface from the chat.
- `one-visual-idea.md`: pick the one picture the tool is built around (seat map, receipt, ticket, graph, board), or decide plain rows are right.
- `meta-visual-language.md`: colour, type, shape, buttons and how loud a picture may be in a Meta rich message; the critic's reference.
- `chat-card-layout.md`: height budget, summary at rest, designed empty and overloaded states, width.
- `people-in-the-picture.md`: faces placed where the action is, the viewer findable, roles, members joining and leaving.
- `motion-with-meaning.md`: motion only to show what changed and who did it; the one satisfying moment; random results from `confirmed`.
- `copy-voice.md`: every visible string in Meta's voice, with before and after pairs; no banter on anyone's behalf.
- `filling-from-chat.md`: real names, options and amounts from the chat with sources; never invent facts; what to do when the chat gives nothing.
- `state-that-verifies.md`: small state, one sentence per action, strong invariants and transitions, arbitrary that explores; passes the gate first time.
- `games-people-finish.md`: turn-based or simultaneous games with a clear end, a visible turn, one-tap moves and a stake.
- `drawing-primitives.md`: the art kit in `@muse/app` (Scene, Placed, SeatMap, Graph, Ticket, Receipt, Thumb, charts, Canvas) and how to combine them.

Exemplars: `exemplars/INDEX.md` lists design notes for tools that show these skills at their best.
