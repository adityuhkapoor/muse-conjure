# Two truths and a lie

**The moment.** An icebreaker or a lore game: three claims about one friend, one is false.

**The visual idea.** The friend's face and "Which one about Theo is the lie?", three claim rows
as soft pills with a tick each, and the faces of who has guessed. A counter on top ("1 of 2").

**Why this shape.** One question at a time, one tap to answer; faces of who guessed make it social
without revealing answers early.

**The satisfying moment.** The reveal: the lie row turns and the right guessers' faces light up.

**Empty and overloaded.** "Be the first to guess"; with many guessers the facepile caps.

**Watch for.** The truths must be facts the chat states; when the chat gives none, the people
write their own claims (a secret that only shows when revealed). Slang in copy ("cap") is the
group's word, keep it out of buttons.
