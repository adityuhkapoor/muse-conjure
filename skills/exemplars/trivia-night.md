# Trivia night

**The moment.** A group night with two teams, and the questions are about the friends themselves.

**The visual idea.** A scoreboard band (team faces, names and big scores), then "About Dev" and a
question built from a chat fact, four answer tiles in a grid, and a large round "Buzz" button with
a status panel ("Leo has the floor · Late Again is locked out").

**Why this shape.** A quiz about us is a game show; the buzzer and the locked-out team give it
turns and stakes that a plain poll lacks.

**The satisfying moment.** Buzzing in: the floor goes to you and the other team's panel greys.

**Empty and overloaded.** "1 of 5" from the start; with more people, teams show three faces and
"+N".

**Watch for.** Questions and answers only from facts people stated in the chat; the buzzer
winner is whoever the server orders first.
