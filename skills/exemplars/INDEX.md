# Exemplars

Design notes for tools that look made for their moment. Each note says the moment, the visual
idea and why this shape, and points at the source. Borrow the thinking, not the content. The
compose pipeline retrieves notes by these lines and tags.

| Note | Shape | The visual idea |
|---|---|---|
| `seat-map.md` | match | real seat rows under a stage, faces in seats |
| `boarding-pass.md` | gather | a pass that fills seats and locks at four |
| `availability.md` | schedule | a week heat grid prefilled from the chat |
| `places-thumbs.md` | choose | place tiles with drawn thumbnails, price and distance |
| `ticket-split.md` | divide | tier cards, the seat block, who's in, the share each |
| `slot-machine.md` | choose | three reels that never land on a veto |
| `settle-up.md` | divide | a tangle of IOU arrows that untangles |
| `rent-split.md` | divide | a floor plan with a price in each room |
| `tab-claim.md` | divide | a receipt you claim line by line |
| `movie-bracket.md` | choose | one matchup at a time with posters and quotes |
| `chore-draft.md` | match | a snake draft board with a fairness load row |
| `dinner-veto.md` | choose | rows struck out with the vetoer's face |
| `bring-list.md` | match | a potluck list that flags doubles and gaps |
| `trivia-night.md` | play | a game-show buzzer on questions about the group |
| `two-truths.md` | play | three claims about a friend, spot the lie |
| `daily-puzzle.md` | play | a word game board with a keyboard and friends' grids |
| `trip-story.md` | remember | a story recap with a drawn skyline and reactions |
| `bill-roulette.md` | choose | a small weighted wheel, one button (a tiny tool) |
| `price-check.md` | gather | a price track and gauge for a Marketplace offer |

Not yet covered by a gate-passing port: a carpool picture and a canvas drawing game.
