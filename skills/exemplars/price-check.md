# Price check

**The moment.** Someone is about to buy a used bike on Marketplace and asks friends whether $350
is fair.

**The visual idea.** A price track with tick marks for nearby sales, a green band where friends
think it is fair, the ask in red, and my guess as a draggable knob. Under it, a half-gauge
("Overpaying" in red) beside the offer in big type with −10 and +10, and the offer drafted as an
outgoing message bubble with one primary "Send $350".

**Why this shape.** Fairness is a position on a line, so the line is the picture. Drafting the
message in a Messenger bubble connects the decision to the action it leads to.

**The satisfying moment.** Nudging the offer moves the needle on the gauge out of the red.

**Empty and overloaded.** Without friends' guesses the band is hidden and the comps carry it.

**Watch for.** Comps and prices are facts from the listing or chat, never invented; sending stays
a person's tap.
