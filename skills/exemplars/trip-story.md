# Trip story

**The moment.** Back from four days in Nashville; the group wants to relive it.

**The visual idea.** A story frame with progress segments on top, a big title ("4 days in
Nashville"), a drawn night skyline with a moon, the friends' faces standing in the city, and a
reaction row (heart, laugh, fire) with counts. Each tap moves to the next card.

**Why this shape.** A recap is a story, and Meta already has the format people know. One fact per
card keeps it light; reactions let people respond without writing.

**The satisfying moment.** Reactions floating up from the tap and counts rising as friends react.

**Empty and overloaded.** The cover works with no reactions; more than four people show as "+1".

**Watch for.** Every card's fact comes from the chat; a recap never invents a memory.
